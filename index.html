Architecture practice

Architecture as a system of decisions, feedback, and constraints.

My approach connects domain modeling to delivery and operations. Diagrams and patterns matter, but they only create value when reflected in code, pipelines, observability, security, and team workflows.

01

Model the domain before the framework

Use bounded contexts, explicit contracts, value objects, and language that matches the business problem.

02

Make quality attributes concrete

Resilience, performance, security, maintainability, and operability must become design constraints and verifiable engineering practices.

03

Automate governance where it helps

Templates, analyzers, CI policies, package validation, repository inspection, and code-review automation turn standards into repeatable feedback.

04

Design for production, not just delivery

Observability, failure modes, idempotency, deployment behavior, and operational ownership are architecture concerns from the beginning.

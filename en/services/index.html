Approach

Understand the system before proposing changes.

A technical decision can solve one problem while creating another. That is why I look at the current system and project constraints before comparing possible approaches.

01 / Context

Understand the context

I identify goals, key dependencies, business constraints, and risks in the existing system.

02 / Alternatives

Make trade-offs explicit

I compare approaches and discuss their impact on maintenance, operations, costs, and the system's ability to evolve.

03 / Evolution

Define next steps

I organize recommendations and next steps, with priorities and evaluation criteria suited to the agreed scope.

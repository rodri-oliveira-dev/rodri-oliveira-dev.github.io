Approach

Understand the decision before choosing a technology.

No architectural solution is right for every context. The work starts with understanding the problem and its constraints rather than imposing a rigid methodology.

01 / Context

Understand the context

Identify goals, the existing architecture, business constraints, risks, and the team's needs.

02 / Alternatives

Make trade-offs explicit

Compare options and their technical consequences, including complexity, operating costs, and future adaptability.

03 / Evolution

Define next steps

Organize decisions, priorities, and verifiable measures suited to the system's current stage.
